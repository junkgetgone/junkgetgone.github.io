Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers about pricing, scheduling and what we haul.

Get my free quoteCall (850) 400-6090
How much does junk removal cost?

We price by how much space your items take up in our truck. Text us a photo and we'll send an upfront price.

Can you come today?

Often, yes. Same-day and next-day pickups are available depending on the schedule.

Do I have to carry anything out?

No. Just point to it — our crew does all the lifting and loading.

Do I need to be home?

Not always. For many jobs you can leave items in an accessible spot or give us access, and we'll text you when it's done.

What areas do you serve?

Panama City Beach, Inlet Beach, Rosemary Beach, Santa Rosa Beach, Miramar Beach, Sandestin and Destin.

What can't you take?

Hazardous materials like paint, chemicals, fuel, asbestos, medical waste and propane tanks. See our What We Take page for details.

Do you donate items?

Yes. Usable furniture and household goods go to local charities whenever possible, and we recycle what we can.

Do you work with businesses and property managers?

Yes — offices, retail, vacation rentals, condos and rental turnovers. Ask about recurring pickups.

Do you do storm cleanup?

Yes. We help clear storm debris, branches and damaged materials after bad weather.

How do I get a quote?

Text a photo to (850) 400-6090, use the quote form, or call us.

Still have a question?

Call or text — we're happy to help.
